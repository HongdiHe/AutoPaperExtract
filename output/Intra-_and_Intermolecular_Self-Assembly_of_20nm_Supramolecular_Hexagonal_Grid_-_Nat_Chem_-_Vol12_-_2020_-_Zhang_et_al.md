## 摘要

The spontaneous self-assembly of multiple distinct building blocks is ubiquitous in nature and underlies the biosynthesis of numerous functional proteins and protein complexes. Inspired by the precision of nature, supramolecular chemists have exploited the highly directional and predictable features of metal coordination to construct an impressive variety of metallo-supramolecular structures. However, bottom-up approaches have mainly given rise to either discrete assemblies smaller than 10 nm or infinite coordination-based polymers. Meso-sized (10-100 nm) discrete architectures with specific sizes and shapes are rare. Here we describe discrete two-dimensional ensembles in the 20-nm-size domain produced by coordination-driven self-assembly, combining intra- and intermolecular self-assembly to prepare a 20-nm-wide metallo-supramolecular hexagonal grid with its eight isomers based on different substructure orientations. The initial intramolecular self-assembly process, reminiscent of the folding process of peptides or proteins, occurs on complexation with Fe2+ metal ions, followed by subsequent intermolecular self-assembly governed by coordination with additional Fe2+ ions. The hexagonal grid was characterized by ESI-MS, TWIM-MS, NMR spectroscopy, and scanning tunneling microscopy at submolecular resolution.

## 主要内容

### 研究背景

配位驱动自组装已成功构建了多种金属超分子结构，但大多数离散组装体尺寸小于10 nm。介观尺度（10-100 nm）的离散结构极为罕见，这主要由于设计困难、组装过程复杂以及表征挑战。

### 研究方法

- 设计了含Ru(II)预组装连接和游离tpy臂的构建单元3
- 分步自组装策略：先通过分子内自组装（配体3与1当量Fe2+形成中间体4），再通过分子间自组装（加入2当量Fe2+形成六角网格5）
- 类蛋白质折叠的分子内组装过程
- 表征手段：ESI-MS（29+至66+电荷态）、TWIM-MS、DOSY NMR、扫描隧道显微镜(STM)

### 关键结果

- 成功构建了直径约20 nm、分子量约65,790 Da的二维离散六角网格结构5
- 这是目前报道的最大的2D离散金属超分子之一
- ESI-MS检测到29+至66+的一系列电荷态峰
- TWIM-MS给出单组信号，表明为高刚性体系
- STM以亚分子分辨率成像了六角中间体和最终网格结构
- 鉴定了由tpy-金属-tpy连接方向不同引起的14种六角网格异构体
- DOSY显示扩散系数D = 7.08 × 10⁻¹¹，logD = -10.15，与单一离散物种一致

### 结论

通过结合分子内和分子间自组装策略，成功构建了20 nm尺度的离散六角网格结构。该工作将配位驱动自组装推进到介观尺度，并通过STM实现了亚分子分辨率的异构体鉴定。

## 涉及的配体/金属/结构

- **配体类型**: 含Ru(II)预组装连接的多齿tpy构建单元(3)
- **金属离子**: Fe²⁺（组装金属）、Ru²⁺（预组装连接）
- **组装结构**: 20 nm六角网格(hexagonal grid)、中间体4、八种异构体
