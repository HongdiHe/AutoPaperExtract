## 摘要

In this study, we overcame a challenge in conventional self-assembly of macrocycles that uses ditopic 2,2':6',2''-terpyridine (tpy) building blocks with a 120 degree angle between two ligating moieties, which generally produces a mixture of multiple macrocycles instead of a single hexagon. Two supramolecular hexagon wreaths, [Zn9LA6] and [Zn12LB6], were designed and self-assembled from tritopic and tetratopic tpy ligands with Zn(II) ions, respectively. These multitopic ligands, bearing multiple binding sites, increased the total density of coordination sites and provided high geometric constraints to induce the formation of discrete structures. Such hexagon wreaths, which were constructed by simple recursion of small hexagons around a central hexagon, exhibit fractal geometry features with self-similarity at different levels. The shapes, sizes, and structures were fully characterized by NMR, ESI-MS, traveling-wave ion mobility mass spectrometry (TWIM-MS), and transmission electron microscopy. With diameters around 5.5 nm for [Zn9LA6] and 5.8 nm for [Zn12LB6], the remarkable rigidity of these fractal architectures was supported by TWIM-MS, in contrast to the high flexibility of macrocycles assembled by ditopic tpy ligands.

## 主要内容

### 研究背景

配位驱动自组装中，使用120度二齿三联吡啶(tpy)配体构建大环时通常会产生多种环状混合物（五聚体至九聚体），无法获得单一的六角形结构。提高配位位点密度(DOCS)是实现离散结构精准自组装的潜在策略。

### 研究方法

- 设计合成了三齿(LA)和四齿(LB)tpy配体
- 通过Sonogashira偶联反应合成配体
- 配体与Zn(NO3)2·6H2O按化学计量比在CHCl3/MeOH中55°C反应12小时
- 表征手段：1H NMR、2D-COSY、温度依赖性NMR、ESI-MS、TWIM-MS、TEM

### 关键结果

- 成功自组装了两个离散的六角花环结构：[Zn9LA6]（直径约5.5 nm）和[Zn12LB6]（直径约5.8 nm）
- TWIM-MS显示每个电荷态仅有单一窄漂移时间分布，证明产物为单一组分
- 与二齿配体8的自组装对比，后者产生五聚体到九聚体的混合物，而三齿/四齿配体产生单一的花环结构
- 花环结构具有分形几何特征——小六角形围绕中心六角形递归排列，表现出不同层次的自相似性
- TWIM-MS证实花环结构具有显著刚性，与二齿tpy配体组装的大环的高柔性形成鲜明对比

### 结论

通过引入多齿tpy配体增加配位位点密度，成功克服了120度二齿tpy配体自组装仅产生混合物的难题，实现了离散分形花环结构的精准构建。这一"配位位点密度"策略为构建更复杂的超分子结构提供了新思路。

## 涉及的配体/金属/结构

- **配体类型**: 三齿三联吡啶配体(LA)、四齿三联吡啶配体(LB)、120度二齿三联吡啶配体(8, 对照)
- **金属离子**: Zn²⁺
- **组装结构**: 六角花环(hexagon wreath)分形结构，[Zn9LA6]和[Zn12LB6]
